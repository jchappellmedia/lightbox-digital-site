Traffic, bounce rate & clicks

Checking…

Two pieces are involved. Tag Manager (GTM-…) is the delivery van; Analytics (G-…) is the thing that actually counts. The van is parked out front — it just has nothing to deliver yet. Getting the Measurement ID finishes it:

  1. Open analytics.google.com and create a property for lightbox-digital.com.
  2. Copy the Measurement ID — it looks like G-XXXXXXXXXX.
  3. Send it to Claude, or paste it into GA4_ID at the top of build.py.

Data starts from the day it's connected — it can't look backwards, so earlier traffic is gone either way.

Once connected, here's where each answer lives. Everything in the right column is already coded on this site.

What you want to knowWhere it shows up
How many people came, and from where Reports › Acquisition › Traffic acquisition — splits Google, Instagram, direct
Bounce rate Reports › Engagement › Pages and screens — GA calls it engagement rate; bounce is the mirror of it
What people click Reports › Engagement › Events — email_click, cta_click, video_play, generate_lead
Which films actually get watched Events › video_play — each row is a film title
How many turned into an email Events › generate_lead (form sent) and email_click (wrote directly)
Which pages people land on and leave from Reports › Engagement › Landing page
